FLAGSHIP · MADANDIH, PURULIA

SIPL Madandih

About SIPL Madandih

Shakambhari Ispat & Power Limited, the flagship company of the Group, operates an Integrated Steel Plant at Madandih, Purulia, West Bengal, spread over an area of more than 150 acres.

The plant comprises Sponge Iron making DRI Kilns, Ferro Alloys, Steel Melting Electrical Furnaces, Billet Casting, a Continuous Casting Machine, 3 fully continuous rolling mills with Belgian-designed TEMPCORE technology, Wire Drawing Machines, a Captive Power Plant and a Quality Assurance Cell with modern apparatus.

ALUMINIUM · MOGRA, HOOGHLY

SIPL Mogra, India Foils

About SIPL Mogra, India Foils

Located in Hoera, Mogra (Hooghly District), the Mogra facility was acquired and modernized by the Shakambhari Group to establish India Foils Limited.

Under the group's "Metal to Foil" initiative, India Foils manufactures precision-rolled ultra-thin aluminium foils ranging from 9 to 60 microns, serving global pharmaceutical, FMCG, food container, and flexible packaging markets.

SPS Steels · Unit 1

About SPS Steels · Unit 1

SPS Steels Rolling Mills Limited (Unit 1) is an Integrated Steel Plant located at the heart of the steel city Durgapur, West Bengal.
