WHAT IS IT?

A Steel Shelf Bracket, At Structural Scale

A structural angle is a long piece of hot-rolled steel shaped like a perfect 90-degree letter "L". Both sides (called "legs") are equal in width. It acts like a strong steel shelf bracket, providing stiffness along corners and joins where two flat surfaces meet.

Rolled directly from pure steel billets, our angles feature crisp 90° corners with clean surfaces that allow for effortless arc welding and bolting without surface prep.

PRIMARY PURPOSE & BEST USE CASES

From Window Grills to Transmission Towers

Equal angles are used whenever a builder needs to join two walls, create a diagonal cross-brace, or build a rigid skeletal frame.

Light (50×50 MM to 65×65 MM)

Best for window grills, door frames, metal shelving units, and light roof trusses.

Medium (75×75 MM to 90×90 MM)

Best for industrial warehouse roof frames, factory shed purlins, and solar panel racking.

Heavy (100×100 MM to 130×130 MM)

Best for high-voltage electricity transmission towers, telecom towers, crane supports, and heavy machinery chassis.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Structural Angles, Common Questions

Which angle size should I use for a warehouse roof frame?

Medium sizes, 75×75mm to 90×90mm, are the standard choice for industrial warehouse roof frames, factory shed purlins and solar panel racking.

Do structural angles come with mill test certificates?

Yes, every bulk shipment of our structural angles comes complete with official Mill Test Certificates confirming chemical composition and mechanical strength tests.

What thickness options exist for the 100×100mm angle?

100×100mm is available in 6, 8, 10 and 12mm thickness, the widest thickness range in our equal-angle series.
