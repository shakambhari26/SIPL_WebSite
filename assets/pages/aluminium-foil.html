ABOUT US

Metal to Foil, Under One Roof

India Foils is Shakambhari Group's aluminium arm, precision-rolling foil from 9 to 60 microns at SIPL Mogra in Hoera, Hooghly. What started as a steel and ferro alloys group extended into aluminium under the same discipline: in-house rolling, annealing and coating, tested before it ever reaches a customer's line.

Recognised as a 4-Star Export House, the facility supplies pharmaceutical, food, FMCG and industrial customers with foil built to exact alloy, temper and thickness specification, not a generic roll cut to size.

OUR MANUFACTURING PROCESS

From Raw Aluminium to Finished Roll

A six-stage protocol, rolled, annealed, slit, coated, printed and inspected before anything is packed for dispatch.

1. Cold Rolling

Raw aluminium is reduced on our Achenbach mill through successive cold-rolling passes, down to gauges from 9 to 100 microns.

2. Annealing

Rolled foil is annealed on a Wesman furnace under controlled atmosphere, setting the soft, semi-hard or hard temper each product needs.

3. Slitting & Rewinding

Master rolls are slit to the exact width ordered, from 50mm tagger stock to 1040mm container-grade foil, and rewound onto cores.

4. Coating & Lamination

Foil is coated on a Rotomec VMCH line or laminated with LDPE, PET or paper for moisture-barrier and heat-seal performance.

5. Printing

Printed grades run up to five-colour rotogravure or flexographic printing with automatic registration, using food-grade inks.

6. Inspection & Packing

Finished rolls are checked for alloy, temper, thickness and width, video-inspected for defects, then packed for dispatch.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Aluminium Foil, Common Questions

What thickness range do you produce?

From 9 to 60 microns across our full range. Pharma and household foils sit toward the thinner end, while semi-rigid container and tagger foils run toward the thicker end, up to 100 microns for tagger stock.

Do you supply printed and branded foil?

Yes. Printed pharmaceutical foil is available in up to five-colour rotogravure or flexographic printing, with automatic registration control and 100% video inspection for defects.

What alloys and tempers do you work in?

Primarily AA8011 and AA8079 for pharma and lidding foil, AA1200 for household foil, AA3003 for semi-rigid container foil, and AA1235 for tagger foil, produced in hard, soft and semi-hard tempers depending on the product.

Is your foil safe for direct food contact?

Yes. Household, semi-rigid container and lidding foils are manufactured for direct food contact, and inks used on printed foil are food-grade and migration-compliant.

How is the foil packed for dispatch?

Finished rolls are wound onto cores and packed as jumbo rolls, ranging from around 100 kg for household foil up to 700 kg for pharma and lidding foil, depending on the product.

Where is this foil manufactured?

At SIPL Mogra, our India Foils facility in Hoera, Hooghly, West Bengal, the same integrated facility handling rolling, annealing, coating and printing under one roof.
