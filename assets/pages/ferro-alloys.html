ABOUT US

Engineering Excellence in Ferro Alloys

We are a premier manufacturer and global exporter of high-quality ferro alloys, specializing in Silico Manganese and High Carbon Ferro products. With a robust production capacity of 20,000 metric tons per month, we are dedicated to supplying the essential elements that make modern steel stronger, cleaner, and more durable.

Our state-of-the-art facility is powered by eleven Submerged Arc Furnaces (SAF), allowing us to maintain continuous, high-volume production without compromising on the precise chemical compositions our clients demand.

GLOBAL FOOTPRINT

A Globally Recognized Partner

We are a globally recognized partner. Currently, approx. 60% of our products are exported to international markets, with the remaining approx. 40% supplied to domestic customers. We are particularly proud of our continuous supply of specialized Low Boron Silico Manganese to steelmakers worldwide, a testament to our manufacturing precision.

OUR MANUFACTURING PROCESS

The Carbothermic Reduction Process

We utilize a highly controlled Carbothermic Reduction Process to ensure the highest grade of alloy extraction. Our modernized facility allows for seamless material handling from raw ore to final dispatch.

1
Step 01

Raw Material Sourcing & Handling

Quality begins at the source. We utilize high and medium-grade manganese ore, combined with premium reductants including coke, coal, and dolomite. Materials are fed into storage bunkers via an automated belt conveyor system.

2
Step 02

Automated Batch Charging

Precision is managed from our central control room. A programmable logic controller (PLC) system seamlessly regulates the exact ratios of raw materials, dropping batches into the furnace charging hoppers for optimal chemical balance.

3
Step 03

The Carbothermic Process (Smelting)

The charge is fed into our Submerged Arc Furnaces. Heavy electric currents are passed through three massive carbon electrodes, generating the intense heat required to reduce the manganese ore into molten alloy and slag.

4
Step 04

Tapping & Separation

After reaching target power consumption, tapping is initiated by opening the tap hole via oxygen lancing. The liquid metal and slag flow through a sophisticated runner and skimmer system. The lighter slag is diverted to a sand bed, while the pure molten alloy is channeled into casting moulds.

5
Step 05

Cooling & Sizing

The liquid metal cools in designated metal beds. Once solidified, the metal casts are transferred to the finished yard, where they are crushed and broken into specific lump sizes tailored to individual client order requirements.

QUALITY ASSURANCE & PACKAGING

Uncompromising Quality Control

Quality is not just a promise; it is a verifiable metric in every batch we produce.

In-House Lot Testing

Once sized, the metal is grouped into designated lots and rigorously tested by our internal quality assurance team.

Third-Party Pre-Inspection

Before any material leaves our facility, an independent third party takes samples and conducts powder testing in their own laboratories to guarantee unbiased certification.

SECURE GLOBAL DISPATCH

Once approved, the finished ferro alloys are securely packed into heavy-duty 1 MT (Metric Ton) Jumbo Bags. Our streamlined dispatch operations ensure that our 8,500 tons of monthly exports reach global ports safely, efficiently, and exactly to spec.
