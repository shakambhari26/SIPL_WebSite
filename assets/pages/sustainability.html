OUR APPROACH

Excellence Built Into Every Stage

Sustainability starts on the plant floor, in an integrated steel plant where raw material and finished product share the same roof, not in a report filed once a year.

Integrated, Efficient Manufacturing

Forward and backward integration means the entire chain, from raw material to finished product, runs under one roof, cutting the waste and transport of a fragmented supply chain.

Green Pro Certified Products

Our Elegant Steel QST rebars carry Green Pro Ecolabel certification from the CII-Green Products & Services Council, verifying sustainable product quality against an independent benchmark.

Quality Checked at Every Stage

Finished products move through several test procedures and quality checkpoints before dispatch, so what leaves the plant is what was promised at the order desk.

Growth That Creates Local Jobs

As our network of plants and group companies expands, it becomes a growing source of employment for the youth and job seekers in the communities around us.

ENVIRONMENT

Built to Outlast Its Own Footprint

Environmental performance isn't a report we file once a year, it's reviewed by a board-level committee and verified by independent certification.

Our Commitment

Conservation of natural resources, preservation of biodiversity and the abatement of climate-change impact, Shakambhari Group's stated aim is to set the steel industry's benchmark in environmental performance.

ISO 14001:2015 Certified

Our mining and manufacturing locations operate an Environmental Management System independently certified to ISO 14001:2015, reviewed by a board-level Safety, Health & Environment Committee.

Climate Responsibility

Steel is considered a hard-to-abate sector globally, but its infinite recyclability makes it part of the long-term solution to climate change, a responsibility reflected in how our plants are run.

Green Pro Certified

Independently certified by CII-Green Products & Services Limited, the Green Pro mark verifies our environmental performance against a national benchmark, not just our own claims.

GOVERNANCE

Accountability Built Into the Boardroom

Ethical conduct and long-term shareholder value aren't in tension at Shakambhari Group, our governance structure is built to deliver both, for every stakeholder.

Ethical, transparent conduct in every transaction, the standard we hold ourselves to in order to protect stakeholder interest and build long-term value.
An Audit Committee reviews financial reporting and internal controls, reporting its findings directly to the Board of Directors.
A Finance Committee oversees financial planning and capital allocation decisions on behalf of the Board.
A Shareholders'/Investors' Grievance Committee gives shareholders and investors a direct channel to the Board.
EMPLOYEE SAFETY

A Safe Workplace, By Design

Safety at Shakambhari Group is treated as an operating priority, not a compliance checkbox, because the people running our furnaces and rolling mills are the reason the plant runs at all.

01

Regular safety training keeps every worker, from the shop floor to the control room, current on procedure and hazard awareness.

02

Every worker, employee and visitor on-site is equipped with the right personal protective equipment (PPE) before stepping onto the plant floor.

03

Scheduled inspection of plant machinery catches wear and risk before it becomes an incident.

04

Safety guidelines are built with the participation of employees at every level, so standards are followed, not just posted on a wall.
