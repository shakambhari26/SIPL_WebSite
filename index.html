Forged to Last

QUALITY

Quality Without Compromise

Eight checkpoints, zero shortcuts, from the moment raw material arrives to the moment a lot clears dispatch, every batch is verified before it moves on.

01 · LAB TEST

Incoming Material Analysis

Every incoming batch of iron ore, coal and dolomite is sampled and tested against a defined chemical benchmark, nothing moves to production until it clears that bar.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
02 · LIVE MONITORING

DRI Live Monitoring & Lab Analysis

Temperature and feed rate are watched continuously while the kiln runs, and the sponge iron that comes out still has to pass its own lab check before it counts as finished.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
03 · LAB TEST

SMS Lab Analysis

Sponge iron, pig iron and ferro alloys each have to be individually verified, all three are checked before anything is cleared into the furnace.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
04 · PROCESS CONTROL

Alloy Dosing & Lollypop Sampling

Once slag is removed, the crude steel is refined and dosed with alloys to a precise ratio, verified with a quick sample pulled straight from the melt.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
05 · LAB TEST

Billet Inspection & Documentation

What comes out of continuous casting gets checked from every angle, look, temperature and composition, with every result logged before the billet is cleared for rolling.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
06 · LIVE MONITORING

Rolling Process Monitoring

Rolling runs under continuous temperature and pressure supervision, every billet tracked in, cooling water watched throughout, and every bar tracked back out.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
07 · LAB TEST

Finished Material Testing

Before anything ships, the finished product is tested across every property that matters: physical, mechanical and chemical, and the results are documented against that batch.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →
08 · DISPATCH

Lot Tagging & Dispatch Clearance

Every lot is tagged, logged and cleared before dispatch, so any bar that leaves the plant can be traced straight back to the batch it came from.

VIEW CHECKPOINT →

OUR PROCESS

From Raw Earth to Reinforced Tomorrow

One continuous chain: our own mines, our own reduction, our own furnace, our own hands, nearly nothing bought as a finished input we could make ourselves.

CHAPTER I

The Earth Gives

Iron ore fines and coal begin the chain, coal drawn straight from Shakambhari's own mines, not rented piece by piece from the market.

VIEW STAGE →
CHAPTER II

Shaped for Purpose

Raw fines are agglomerated into uniform spherical pellets, engineered for the permeability clean reduction demands.

VIEW STAGE →
SPONGE IRON · KILN

The Stripping Away

Inside the rotary kiln, oxygen is stripped from the pellets at ~950°C, no coking, no blast furnace, just metallic sponge iron.

VIEW STAGE →
~1600°C · EAF

Where It Becomes One

Sponge iron, scrap, pig iron and in-house silicomanganese melt in the induction furnace into one liquid steel.

VIEW STAGE →
CONTINUOUS CASTING

Discipline Takes Form

Liquid steel is drawn continuously through water-cooled molds into billets, disciplined enough to hold whatever shape comes next.

VIEW STAGE →
ROLLING MILL

Pressure Reveals Purpose

Billets are reheated and rolled under pressure into TMT bars, wire rods and structural steel, each built to hold something up.

VIEW STAGE →
